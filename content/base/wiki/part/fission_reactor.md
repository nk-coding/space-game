The fission reactor is the first power source most ship-minds build. It is small enough to tuck between
armor blocks, and it feeds the shared power pool of the piece it sits in: every part in that piece draws
from the same pool, with no wiring.

## Keeping it cool

A reactor makes heat in proportion to how hard the piece is drawing on it. Put a [[part:radiator]] beside it
on a side that faces open space: the radiator dumps heat *and* raises the reactor's output while it vents.
A radiator buried inside the hull does neither.

## When it breaks off

If the reactor ends up in a piece that has broken away from the ship, that piece keeps its own pool. Parts
left behind without a reactor go dark.

## When it is destroyed

A destroyed reactor explodes. Every part in the eight cells around it takes the blast at once: machines with
one hit point, such as a gun or a [[part:logic_core]] right beside it, go with it, and a reactor beside it goes
off in turn. Armor takes only part of a blast and lets only that part through, so plating around a reactor
usually survives it and shields what lies behind it. A reactor in front of a gun is no shield for it.

The blast does not stop at the hull: a ship alongside, a wreck or a piece that broke off takes it too, unless
its own [[part:shield_emitter|shield]] holds it back. Your ship's own shields never do: the blast goes off
inside them.
