# GDR-0030: No economy: no credits, no trading; parts keep build-point costs for balance

- Status: Accepted
- Date: concept phase (exact day not recorded; settled by 2026-10-11)
- Decided by: Niklas
- Related: [GDR-0004](0004-grid-ship-building-no-wiring.md), [GDR-0029](0029-mild-death-bonded-matter.md), [GDR-0031](0031-materials-and-fabrication.md), [GDR-0032](0032-balancing-arena-evolution.md)

## Context

Progress comes from where you have been (materials) and what you have researched, not from money.

## Options considered

- **Credits and trading**: rejected; reason not recorded.

## Decision

- No credits, no trading.
- Every part still has a build-point cost. It keeps arena fights fair and later scales enemy difficulty; designs
  are built within a build-point budget.

## Consequences

- Materials and research are the only progression currencies (GDR-0025, GDR-0031).
- Build points are balance numbers, not prices (placeholders today).
