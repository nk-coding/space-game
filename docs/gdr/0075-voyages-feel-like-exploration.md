# GDR-0075: Voyages should feel like exploring a galaxy and following hints, not a fixed path

- Status: Accepted (direction; the design is open)
- Date: 2026-10-14
- Decided by: Niklas (in chat, while the voyage map got zoom and a portal transition)
- Related: [GDR-0026](0026-procedural-galaxy-and-bands.md), [GDR-0028](0028-clusters-and-voyages.md), [GDR-0062](0062-first-playable-loop.md), [GDR-0065](0065-phase-7-screens-and-escort-action.md), `docs/plans/galaxy-voyages.md`

## Context

The first playable loop (GDR-0062, GDR-0065) has four hand-made rim voyages in a chain: each one opens the next,
ending at the Warden, and the voyage map shows that chain. That was right for a slice, but it reads as a fixed path.

## Decision

Niklas, 14 Oct 2026: "the whole voyages thing should really feel more like exploring a galaxy and following some
hints, less like following a fixed path".

This is a direction for every later voyage and galaxy work, not a design yet:
- The galaxy is something to explore: uncharted space, places found rather than handed out, more than one way on.
- What leads the player is hints (signals, lore fragments, scans, rumours from derelicts and stations) pointing at
  places worth a voyage, not a list of the next steps.
- The slice's chain may stay as the first steps, but nothing new should deepen the fixed-path feel (no drawn
  route between voyages, no strictly linear unlocks as the main structure).

## Consequences

- The next galaxy design (docs/plans/galaxy-voyages.md, its section 4 onward) must be reworked from this before it
  becomes records; questions about how hints work go to Niklas interactively.
- The voyage map's zoomed-out galaxy view shows uncharted space as unknown, not as a route.
