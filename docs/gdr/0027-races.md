## Context

The brief asks for lots of content with alien races. Each band of the galaxy (GDR-0026) needs its own foes and its
own tech.

## Options considered

Not recorded.

## Decision

- **Rim: rogue automatons**: feral precursor drones; simple ships in small packs; early foes, rich in salvage.
- **Outer reach: the Grown** (wary): organic hulls that regrow, love ramming with living prows; boss: giant Grown
  ships.
- **Middle reach: the Swarm** (hostile by nature, no standing): drone floods from hive carriers; hives are the
  bosses; raids its neighbours.
- **Inner reach: the Lattice** (neutral, open to standing, the most likely allies): long-range prisms and
  refractive crystal armor; and **Phase walkers** (wary): blink strikes with armor-piercing lances; boss: giant
  phase ships.
- Races exist mainly for variety and tech: each teaches its own tech branch. Access, allies and missions exist but
  stay light. Allies only inside specific race missions.
- Races rarely clash (they stay in their regions); the Swarm is the exception.

## Consequences

- Each race brings its own materials (GDR-0031), tech branch (GDR-0024) and enemy designs (GDR-0032).
