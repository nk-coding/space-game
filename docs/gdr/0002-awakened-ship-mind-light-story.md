# GDR-0002: The player is an awakened ship-mind at a precursor shipyard; the story stays light

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-10
- Decided by: Niklas (interactive Q&A)
- Related: [GDR-0013](0013-vessels-and-piloting.md), GDR-0017, GDR-0026, GDR-0029, GDR-0031

## Context

The brief: build and upgrade ships, use them in a vast world, long playtime with lots of content, AI enemies. A
location-based idea was floated but felt odd.

## Options considered

- **Location-based play**: parked (light version only: your real location seeds your home region).
- Other premises: not recorded.

## Decision

- You are a precursor ship-mind that wakes in an abandoned precursor shipyard at the rim of the galaxy. The
  shipyard is the fixed home: drydock, research lab, fabricator (parts and escorts), test range (simulated fights
  against archived enemies and your own past designs).
- You control ships through logic cores, each hosting a copy of you (GDR-0013).
- The precursors are gone. They built the gates, the guardians, the megastructures, the archive at the core,
  and you.
- First hour: wake as a tiny drone in a dead shipyard → salvage nearby automaton wrecks → repair the drydock →
  build your first real ship → first rim voyage → first research.
- Story stays light: procedural missions and lore on derelicts. Lore also unlocks reverse-engineering.

## Consequences

- The premise carries several mechanics: capture by copying yourself into a dark core (GDR-0017), bonded matter
  re-forming at home (GDR-0029), programmable matter in every logic core (GDR-0031), vessels (GDR-0013).
- Little writing is needed; content variety comes from races, set pieces and procedural missions.
