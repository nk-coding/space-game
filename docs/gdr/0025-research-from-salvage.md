# GDR-0025: Research comes from salvage and scans, and only counts once brought home

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-10
- Decided by: Niklas
- Related: [GDR-0024](0024-tech-trees-no-mk-levels.md), [GDR-0028](0028-clusters-and-voyages.md), [GDR-0029](0029-mild-death-bonded-matter.md), [GDR-0031](0031-materials-and-fabrication.md), `docs/concept-notes.md` "World"

## Context

Loot should be components, and alien wrecks should unlock alien tech. Voyages need a reason to come home.

## Options considered

Other research sources were not decided. Status note: the concept notes' decision table lists Progression as
"Leaning" ("other sources undecided"); the concept board lists the salvage rule as settled, and the 10 Oct world
Q&A decided it. Recorded as `Accepted`, with other sources open.

## Decision

- Dismantling salvaged parts gives research data for that part's family. Alien nodes also need an artifact or lore
  from that race's derelicts.
- Scans give research data too, plus a persistent cluster map, lore and enemy intel (parts and behavior).
- Salvage and scan data only become research once they reach home. Long term, only research matters.
- Salvage: loose debris is picked up by flying through it or with a tractor beam; intact parts on big wrecks have
  to be cut free. Cargo bays are ship parts (cargo 1×1 = 4 units, hold 2×2 = 20, placeholders), so a salvage run
  trades firepower for hold space.

## Consequences

- Death costs part of the haul (GDR-0029), which gives voyages tension.
- Open: other research sources.
