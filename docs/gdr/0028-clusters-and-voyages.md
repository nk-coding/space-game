# GDR-0028: Clusters are large; a default voyage covers one cluster in 15–30 minutes

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-10
- Decided by: Niklas (interactive Q&A)
- Related: [GDR-0017](0017-combat-support-repair-capture-sensors.md), [GDR-0025](0025-research-from-salvage.md), [GDR-0026](0026-procedural-galaxy-and-bands.md), [GDR-0029](0029-mild-death-bonded-matter.md), [ADR-0022](../adr/0022-one-simulation-space-per-cluster.md)

## Context

A long game that should also fit a phone session.

## Options considered

- **Design challenges and puzzles** as a main activity: not chosen.
- Other voyage structures: not recorded.

## Decision

- Clusters are large: 10+ minutes to cross.
- A default voyage covers one cluster and takes 15–30 minutes (phone-friendly). Longer voyage types exist (deep
  expeditions, boss hunts, megastructure dives) but are never forced.
- Activities in clusters: fights, salvage and scanning, light missions from race stations; hazards come with the
  band.
- Travel: cruise inside clusters, warp along lanes for shorter hops, precursor gates for long distances.
- Supplies per voyage: ammo, fuel and repair material; refilled at home; outposts refill and do heavy repairs.
- The loop: refit at the shipyard → gate to the frontier → cruise and warp → fight, salvage, scan → home with the
  haul → research, refit.

## Consequences

- Clusters are the unit of simulation (ADR-0022) and of a play session.
- Autopilot handles transit, manual flying the fights (GDR-0008).
