# GDR-0029: Death is mild: bonded matter re-forms your ship at home; the unbonded haul stays as a wreck

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-10
- Decided by: Niklas (interactive Q&A)
- Related: [GDR-0002](0002-awakened-ship-mind-light-story.md), [GDR-0025](0025-research-from-salvage.md), [GDR-0030](0030-no-economy.md), [GDR-0031](0031-materials-and-fabrication.md)

## Context

Ships are built from many parts and get shot apart. Losing a ship for good would punish building; losing nothing
would remove the tension of a voyage.

## Options considered

Not recorded beyond "mild".

## Decision

- Parts are made of precursor programmable matter. A new part consumes its materials once, bonding its matter to
  your mind.
- When a ship is destroyed, its bonded matter is recalled through the gates and re-forms at home: rebuilding is
  free, and you respawn at home with your ship.
- The haul isn't bonded, so part of it stays behind as a wreck.
- Refitting returns a removed part's matter.
- Salvaged parts can be installed as they are (a small programmable-matter bonding cost) or dismantled for research
  and materials.

## Consequences

- No repair bills and no economy needed for losses (GDR-0030).
- What is at stake on a voyage is the haul, not the ship.
