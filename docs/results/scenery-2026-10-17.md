# Scenery between the sites: the slice with asteroid rock, rams on rock, AI near rock, cost (17 Oct 2026)

For [GDR-0104](../gdr/0104-scenery-rocks-filler-landmarks.md): asteroid rock as anchored bodies in the slice's first
three voyages and every generated cluster type, and what the client draws between the sites. Does the slice's curve
([GDR-0100](../gdr/0100-fight-balance-targets.md)) survive, do ships get stuck, what does it cost.

**In short.**

- With rock the slice keeps its curve (each voyage at most as easy as the one before, 120 seeds) and gets up to 13
  points harder; the Warden's gate holds no rock and is unchanged run for run.
- Unscaled, a ram on an anchored rock carries the ship's whole mass (two to three times a ram on a ship of the same
  mass) and the slice fell up to 19 points, the losses doubling at the first voyage. Scaling what the ship takes by
  the rock (`ram`: iron 0.5, ice 0.35) and better steering brought it to within the noise of a run with no ram damage
  on rock at all.
- No ship stayed pressed on a rock for 3 s in 100 voyage runs through Corran Reach and four cluster types made into
  voyages. The runner's whole voyages step at 0.005–0.009 ms (native release).
- The client's filler is static meshes moved each frame: about 50 entities, 18 tiles, one mesh per rock group.

## Method

The game's headless voyage runner (`sim::voyage::runner`) on every thread through the balance harness's voyage mode
(`../space-game-balance`, `--content` the pack, release build), seeds 1–120 per voyage and fleet: **clear** = goal
cleared and home, **home** = recalled without it, **lost**; no run ran out of time. ± about 8 points at 120 seeds (95 %).
`skiff_cannon.ron` is `ships/rim_skiff.ron` with `pulse_laser` replaced by `reverse_engineered_cannon` (as in
`slice-balance-2026-10-18.md`). "Before" is the same build with the voyages' `scenery` taken out of a copy of the pack
(the runner's steering does nothing without rocks, so it flies exactly as before: its numbers match the 18 Oct report's
first halves). Load average 1.5.

## Rams on rock

A probe over 60 seeds (rim_skiff and Runner at the first and third voyage) counted every contact of the flagship with
a rock. With the first steering (goal only, the nearest rock ahead) a Runner rammed rock 28 times in 60 Corran
Reach runs for 465 hit points, up to 45 at once (2367 kN·s at 19 m/s): it threaded between two rocks, the goal
flipping from one to the other, or coasted into one under its lock (a ship with stern thrust only cannot strafe out
of it). The rim_skiff has 24 hit points; a 15-point ram split it.

The cannon starter at Shipyard approach, clear % as the changes went in (120 seeds; 92 with no rock):

| Build | Clear % |
|---|---|
| first steering, unscaled rams | 76 |
| + the pilot holds fire through rock | 74 |
| test build: no ram damage on rock at all | 85 |
| steering by the first gap entered, three passes, slowing for a gap too narrow, bending a ship's own motion first; the pilot lets go of its lock when carried into rock | 79 |
| + the rock's `ram` factor (iron 0.5, ice 0.35): **the build of record** | 82 |

It lost 4 runs before rock, 13 with the first build, 11 now.

The scripted pilot also fired through rock: 693 shots on rock in 60 runs of the Runner at the first voyage. It now
holds fire with a rock in its line of fire (`Sim::rock_between`) and slides aside: 50.

## Stuck ships

The same probe flew Corran Reach and four cluster types made into voyages (each site at its largest count, the last
one the goal; the type's scenery), 20 seeds each, Runner: 13–28 rocks spawned a run, and no ship (the flagship or an
enemy) touched a rock for 90 ticks in a row. Outcomes: Corran 9 cleared, 9 home, 2 lost; hunting reach 17 home, 3
lost (its packs, not its belt); wreck drift 6 / 13 / 1; quiet field 16 / 4 / 0; scorched deep 6 / 13 / 1.

## Cost

- **Sim** (native release): the runner's whole voyages, a ship against one to five sites with 9–28 rocks spawned,
  step at 0.005–0.009 ms on average including the director and steering. Anchored bodies cost the solver nothing;
  steering is one gap test per spawned rock and steered ship (up to three passes) each tick; rocks spawn by fields as
  sites do (E0: records cost nothing).
- **Client** (`world::scenery`, measured by construction: no GPU on the VM, and SwiftShader's times say nothing): a
  wreck drift spawns 9 glint tiles (264 motes and 26 glints a tile, about 1.3 k vertices), 9 debris tiles (320 bits,
  about 1.1 k vertices), 12–24 debris groups round the sites (10–18 bits each), up to 6 derelicts (about 100 vertices
  each), 1–2 landmarks (a station about 1 k vertices) and one mesh per rock group (20 rocks about 2.4 k vertices).
  Per frame: the transforms of about 50 entities, two twinkle and blink alphas, and while a distant fight shows one
  mesh of up to a few hundred vertices rebuilt. Large translucent layers on screen: the backdrop's star glow and the
  corona (thin strips), so at most two besides the opaque gradient; everything else is small dots and polygons.
