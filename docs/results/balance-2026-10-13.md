# Fight balance after turning v2 (13 Oct 2026)

Re-measurement of the sandbox fight balance after the slower turning of GDR-0055/GDR-0058, with the causes of the
three open balance items of the roadmap (the `raider_basic` knife edge, weak `skirmisher` wingmen, shields that
never collapse), the variants tried and a recommended change set. The change set is proposed in
[GDR-0061](../gdr/0061-fight-balance-after-turning-v2.md); nothing here is applied to the content. (Applied later
the same day together with the sim fixes of GDR-0064, and re-measured: see
[After GDR-0061 and GDR-0064](#after-gdr-0061-and-gdr-0064-13-oct-2026).) Measured on
`dev` `28aa0b5`; the results on `dev` `a213a92` (salvage data, locked beam lasers) are bit-identical. All numbers
in the content and the sim are placeholders; this report only says how they play against each other.

**In short.**

- The 13 Oct turning change made every AI-flown ship-mind ship weaker (it turns its fixed guns onto the target
  half as fast) and the raiders a little clumsier; the player stand-ins are about where they were.
- `raider_basic` orbits at 67% of its cannon's reach = 50.25 m, which is exactly where the Runner's beams stop
  touching a raider (45 m reach from the nose ≈ 50 m centre to centre). At 70% (52.5 m) a passive Runner wins
  **0 of 200** waves instead of 30%. The pulse lasers that the 12 Oct tuning counted on cannot hit a raider the
  nose is pointed at (they sit 5.5 m off the axis; **1–2% hits**).
- The `skirmisher` spends a third of its time in two orbit rules (one fires against every wave of three), in
  which the nose follows the thrust, and the slow turning makes every switch back to the target cost 1.5–3 s:
  a lone Runner fires in only **12%** of the time a raider is within its reach and wins **28%** of the waves.
- Shields collapsed in **0** of the 38 000+ baseline fights: the 2.3 m bubbles take only 26% of the cannon hits
  (the nose with the beams sits outside them), 0.40 per hit, while each regenerates 1.5/s.
- **A sim bug** dominates the AI-vs-AI fights: an AI ship at its top speed can get stuck flying straight on
  forever (raiders spend up to 34% of their time like that). Fixing it makes the skirmisher problem worse, so
  the content changes below are measured on both the current sim and a diagnostic fix.
- Recommended (content only): raider orbit **67% → 58%**, the skirmisher **without its two orbit rules**, shield
  **capacity 6 → 3, regen 1.5 → 0.2**. Lone skirmisher Runner vs a wave **28% → 54%** (fixed sim: 2% → 40%),
  passive pilot 30% → 50% wins, no cliff within ±6% of the orbit radius, shields collapse in 20% of the passive
  pilot's waves, 13% of the lone skirmisher's and 21% of the 3-vs-6 fights.

## Method

The harness is the separate local repo `../space-game-balance` (ADR-0025; see [Reproduce](#reproduce)). It runs
headless fights on the sim's API with the content behaviors, 200 seeds per setup and variant (seeds 1–200), up to
180 s per fight (120 s for the duels). The sim rolls nothing in these setups; the seed only places the spawns, so
every number is deterministic per seed and the same seeds were used before and after every change.

| Setup | What |
|---|---|
| `wave3_passive` | Halvex Runner at rest, piloted by the *passive* stand-in of the 12 Oct measurement (locks the nearest raider and fires, never moves), vs a sandbox wave of 3 raiders spawned as `scene.rs` does (105–125 m out, 0.32 rad apart, 3 m/s inwards, the wave's bearing random per seed) |
| `wave3_closing` | the same with the *closing* stand-in (plus the stick towards the target while it is farther than 45 m, drift on) |
| `wave3_skirm` | the Runner unpiloted under `skirmisher` vs a wave of 3 (the 12 Oct "skirmisher" row) |
| `fleet2_closing` | closing pilot + 2 Runner wingmen under `skirmisher` (sandbox `?fleet=2`) vs a wave of 3 |
| `fleet3_skirm6` | 3 unpiloted Runners vs a wave of 6 |
| `duel_runner` / `duel_early` / `duel_mid` | Runner / Sample early / Sample mid under `skirmisher` vs 1 raider |
| `early3_vs3` | 3 Sample early vs a wave of 3 |
| `load20` | the load test's lines (10 Runners vs 10 raiders, all `raider_basic`), positions jittered by up to 2 m |

A fight is won when the enemy has no *running* vessel left (the sandbox's wave rule), lost when the player side
has none, else it times out; in brackets the timeouts in which no enemy vessel left has a working gun ("disarmed":
won in effect, the sandbox would still wait for the remnants). Win/loss/timeout are given in percent of 200.

Recorded per fight and per class of ship: shots and hits per weapon (also only those fired with the target within
the gun's reach), hull and shield damage, collapses, parts lost by kind, distance to the target in bands (beam,
pulse laser and cannon fire ranges at 93.3%: 42 / 56 / 70 m, the raider's engage range 90 m), time in its own fire
range and firing there, nose on the target, |ω|, time with the last resort on, overshoot, stall, time stuck at
the top speed, maneuver and rule shares. Definitions in the harness README.

### What the turning change did

Diagnostic variant `old_turning`: the content turning numbers before GDR-0055 (gyro 5000, logic core 500,
automaton core 600, RCS 60 kN), the sim code unchanged. Win% (current → old turning numbers): passive 30 → 40,
closing 71 → 58, skirmisher Runner 28 → 31, fleet2 85 → 93, fleet3 18 → 26, Sample early duel 4 → 18, Sample mid
duel 52 → 84, early3_vs3 2 → 29. The last resort runs 27% of the raiders' time (9% with the old core) and 78–83% of
the sample ships' (16–39%). The gyro-less sample ships lost most: their first-line α fell tenfold (cores 500 → 50
kN·m), they turn on differential thrust nearly all the time and get the nose on a raider in 4–5% of the time.

## Causes

### 1. The knife edge: the orbit sits on the beam's edge, and the pulse lasers cannot hit

- `raider_basic` (shares of the cannon's 75 m): stand off at 85% = 64 m, orbit at 67% = 50.25 m for 4 s of every
  9 s. The Runner's beams reach 45 m from muzzles at its nose, about 3 m ahead of its centre of mass, and a raider
  is 3 m wide: a raider's centre must be within ≈ 50 m of the Runner's centre to be touched. The orbit is that
  distance. Raider orbit sweep (current sim, `orbit=NN`, 200 seeds; passive win / loss, closing win):

  | Orbit | 55% | 58% | 60% | 62% | 64% | 67% (now) | 70% | 72% | 75% |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | metres | 41 | 43.5 | 45 | 46.5 | 48 | 50.25 | 52.5 | 54 | 56 |
  | passive win / loss | 50 / 28 | 51 / 22 | 34 / 39 | 42 / 34 | 37 / 36 | 30 / 49 | **0 / 91** | 0 / 92 | 0 / 98 |
  | closing win | 66 | 74 | 72 | 70 | 72 | 71 | 45 | 42 | 42 |

  With the bug fix (below) the curve is the same (67%: 34 / 43, 70%: 2 / 91).
- The 12 Oct tuning expected the pulse lasers (60 m) to punish the orbit. They sit at the wing tips, 5.5 m off the
  axis and parallel to it; a raider is 3 m wide, so a pulse misses whenever the nose points at the raider: 1–2%
  hits for the pilot, 10–15% under fire at will (which fires them only when the raider is 46–56 m out, where
  `atan(5.5 / d)` is inside the 0.12 rad cone). The Runner's real reach against raiders is the beams' 45 m.
- The beams, its only effective weapon, are the first thing shot off: the raiders lead their shots at the Runner's
  centre, the Runner faces them, and the beams sit at the nose outside the shield bubbles. A passive Runner loses
  1.30 of its 2 beams per wave (on average at 24 s), and a Runner without beams cannot kill anything.
- Under the lock the nose trails a crossing raider (see [Sim issues](#sim-issues) 4), so even within reach the
  pilot's beams hit only 12–19% of their ticks.

### 2. The skirmisher fights with its nose off the target

- Rules: flee below 25% hull; orbit 80% when the shield is down; **orbit 90% (54 m) when 3 enemies are within
  100 m** (true for every standard wave); **orbit 60% for the last 4 s of every 10 s** within 120% of reach; face a
  fast attacker; approach 67% (40 m). The lone Runner (wave3_skirm) spends 14% of its time in the crowd orbit,
  12% in the cycle orbit and 50% approaching from afar.
- Orbit and the far approach fly with `HeadingGoal::Auto`: the nose follows the thrust, not the target. A
  Runner with engines only at the back also flips to brake at the end of every approach. Each switch back to
  `face` / approach-at-range is a 90–180° turn: 1.5–3 s at 1.5 s per 90° (GDR-0055; 0.8–1.5 s before). Result:
  firing in 12% of its in-reach time, nose on in 12%, last resort on 20% of the time.
- Raiders (0.57 s per 90°) fire in 43–48% of their in-reach time and hit 88–97% of their shots.
- Split-off vessels (a cored piece of a Runner) count their hull against the whole design's hit points, so they are
  always under 25% and flee at once (75–96% of their time); harmless, but they keep waves from ending.

### 3. Shields never collapse

- Each Runner emitter's 2.3 m bubble sits around a cell 2.5 m behind the nose; the beams and nose slopes are
  outside it. Only 17–35% of the cannon hits cross a bubble; a bubble takes 0.40 per hit (half of a 1.2 shot, less
  after ricochets) and regenerates 1.5/s. Under a wave of three a bubble absorbs about 0.1–0.25 per second on
  average: it never empties. 0 collapses in all baseline runs; the skirmisher's `shield_down` rule never fires.
- Lowering only the regen does nothing (absorption per hit is unchanged, the bubbles were never low): regen 0.5
  gives the same fights, bit for bit.

## Sim issues

Things that look like sim bugs or sim design gaps rather than balance; numbers from the harness, not fixed here.

1. **AI ships stuck at their top speed (bug, since GDR-0055's whole-speed fade).** `Thruster::falloff` zeroes a
   row whenever its push has *any* positive component along the motion and the whole speed is at its rating.
   Maneuver goals routinely ask for more than the top speed (`seek` and `orbit` add the target's velocity, `face`
   and approach-at-range match it; a Runner at 22 m/s outruns a raider's 20). A raider at 20 m/s with its velocity
   ~90° off its nose then asks for a push along its nose: the push is a hair "with" the motion, so it gets zero
   thrust; the heading logic thinks it can push there and never turns. It flies straight on for the rest of the
   fight (traced: 670 m away after 100 s). Raiders spend 34% of their time stuck in wave3_skirm, 19% in
   fleet3_skirm6; most timeouts there are this. Before 13 Oct the forward thruster faded only by the speed along
   its own axis and kept full thrust. Diagnostic fix in the harness repo (`patches/clamp-goal-velocity.diff`):
   clamp every maneuver goal velocity to `Kin::top_speed`. Stuck time drops to ~0 for AI ships, and the fights
   change a lot: wave3_skirm 28/32/40 → **2/70/28**, fleet3_skirm6 18/18/64 → 6/39/56, Sample mid duel 52/2/45 →
   28/16/55 (the stuck raiders were flying away from fights they were winning). The pilot's own case is not
   covered by the clamp: a locked Runner at top speed whose velocity is ~90° off the lock direction gets no
   forward thrust either (the closing stand-in is stuck 5–9% of its time; a player would feel "the ship ignores
   the stick"). A real fix probably wants both: goals capped at the top speed, and a fade that is continuous in the
   angle between push and motion instead of a sign test.
2. **An AI vessel without engines never aims.** With nothing to push, `HeadingGoal::Auto` keeps the heading, so a
   Runner whose tail was shot off drifts with its guns pointing wherever they were (part of the remaining
   timeouts: it drifts at ~21 m/s, faster than the raiders chasing it). Facing the target when no thrust is left
   would let it fight on as a turret.
3. **Fixed parallel guns and small targets.** Guns fire along their facing (GDR-0018), so guns far off the axis
   cannot hit a target smaller than their offset that the nose points at; the Runner's wing pulse lasers are dead
   weight against raiders (1–2% hits). A design question (convergence, or aim the lock for the strongest gun's
   line), not a number.
4. **The lock and `face` lag moving targets.** The heading controller has no feed-forward of the target's bearing
   rate: the steady-state error is `max(ω / turn_gain, ω² / (2 · turn_margin · α))`. With the Runner's first-line
   α of 2.21 rad/s² (GDR-0058) the second term dominates: 1.6° at 0.3 rad/s (traced: the nose 1.4–2.5° behind at
   0.28–0.37 rad/s) and 6.7° at 0.6 rad/s, a raider crossing at 20 m/s 33 m away: 3.9 m off, a clean miss. With
   the old α (9.35) it was 1.4° and 2.9°. A feed-forward of the target's angular rate would remove most of it.
5. Two ships that each fly relative to the other's velocity (approach-at-range, orbit, face) drift together to
   their top speeds (mean speeds 17–19 m/s in the AI-vs-AI fights, raider top 20 m/s); this is what feeds issue 1.
6. Split-off vessels flee at once (hull share against the whole design, see cause 2).

## Reproduce

Harness: the local repo `/home/devbox/workspace/space-game-balance` (Rust, see its README); it depends on
`crates/data` and `crates/sim` of a checkout by path (the main checkout by default).

```sh
CARGO_TARGET_DIR=$PWD/target cargo build --release -j 6
target/release/space-game-balance --variants base,old_turning,rec,rec_b --seeds 1-200 --threads 12 --detail --csv out/final.csv > out/final.md
python3 pivot.py out/final.csv
target/release/space-game-balance --setups wave3_passive,wave3_closing,wave3_skirm,fleet2_closing \
  --variants base,orbit=55,orbit=58,orbit=60,orbit=62,orbit=64,orbit=67,orbit=70,orbit=72,orbit=75 --seeds 1-200 --csv out/orbit.csv
```

`rec` = `orbit=58+sk_lean+cap=3+regen=0.2`, `rec_b` = `rec` with `radius=3.5`. For the "fixed sim" columns apply
`patches/clamp-goal-velocity.diff` to a scratch checkout, point the harness's `Cargo.toml` there and rebuild. The
edited RON files (the recommended set typed into `content/base`, read with `--content`) give bit-identical
results to the `rec` variant (2000 fights compared). 8000 fights take about 20 s on 12 threads (load average
~14 from other agents; the timing does not affect any number).

The section "After GDR-0061 and GDR-0064" was measured with the harness built against the fix branch (both path
dependencies pointed at its worktree), `--variants base` (the content of GDR-0061 is in `content/base` now, so the
`rec` variant's patches no longer match it), and five extra wingman setups (`fleet2_escort`, `fleet2p_skirm`,
`fleet2p_escort`, `fleet2_skirm6`, `fleet2_escort6`) plus a trace and maneuver-share arm for the new
`Maneuver::Escort`; the harness needs that arm to build against the sim from now on. The "without" columns
switched one fix off in a scratch build each. 3000 fights take about 5 s on 8 threads.
