## Radiation: how strong

Every part of a ship takes the radiation's heat per second through each side open to space (the hot star's rule,
GDR-0102, the same everywhere in the cluster). A probe (a throwaway example, not kept) flew each design alone in a
radiating world for 300 s: **idle** (shields up, nothing else) and **fighting** (guns firing 3 s of every 5, engines at
half throttle, shields up: a running fight). Seconds to the first overheated part (– none in 300 s); "bare" is the
design with its radiators swapped for steel blocks.

| Design (fighting) | 0 | 0.1 | 0.15 | 0.2 | 0.25 | 0.3 | 0.35 | 0.4 | 0.6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Halvex Runner (4 radiators) | – | – | – | – | – | – | – (hottest 0.95 of its limit) | 172 | 17 |
| the Runner, bare | – | – | 178 | 172 | 167 | 23 | 18 | 16 | 7 |
| rim skiff (4 radiators) | – | – | – | – | – | – | – | – | 11 |
| `sample_mid` (6 radiators) | – | – | – | – | – | – | – | – | – (0.92) |
| `sample_mid`, bare | – | – | – | – | 172 | 167 | 28 | 17 | 6 |
| precursor sentry, aegis, station, the warden | – | – | – | – | – | – | – | – | aegis 12, the rest – |

Idle, nothing overheats at any of these (the hottest part of the bare Runner at 0.6: 0.96 of its limit). The heat
comes in through the skin and the thick middle of a hull warms over tens of seconds, so a bare ship lasts minutes
where the curve is shallow and seconds past it.

**Chosen** (`cluster_types/core.ron`, every core type): `radiation: (edge: 0.25, centre: 0.35)`. On the core's outer
edge a ship without radiators overheats after about three minutes of fighting (the bare Runner 167 s, the bare mid
sample 172 s); at the galaxy's centre within half a minute (18 s, 28 s). The stock designs with their four to six
radiators cope everywhere (the Runner at the centre runs at 0.95 of its hottest part's limit, so one radiator more is
welcome there); the core's own ships carry radiators along their flanks and cope. A core cluster lies 0 to 1.1 S
from the centre, so its radiation is 0.25 to 0.35 (the Archive's cluster, nearest the centre, the most).

The test `radiation_overheats_a_ship_without_radiators_and_a_cooled_one_copes` (`crates/core/sim/tests/core_band.rs`)
keeps these: the bare Runner overheats within 60 s at the centre's value and only after 60 s (within 300 s) at the
edge's; the Runner and the sentry cope for 300 s at the centre's.

## The galaxy

The core band (edge 1.1 S) holds 2–5 clusters at the galaxy's spacing; a band with a `centre` now needs at least three
or the generator's attempt starts again (seeds 1–200 before the rule: one cluster in 4 galaxies, two in 48). Seeds
1–200 with the rule (`content-tool galaxy --seed N`): three core clusters in 137 galaxies, four in 60, five in 3;
the Archive's trail in all 200 (2 signals in 167, 3 in 33; with three clusters it can walk back through only two),
both core lore fragments in all 200, the spare sealed vault in 63. The generation hash pin moved
`af364f2879722a2d` → `7eb322ae41afb209` (the core's content and the centre; seed 1's layout is unchanged).
`scripts/determinism-check.sh --galaxy 200`: native and wasm32-wasip1 **identical** (`fe26ae25c0fd2919c4bc35b491947143`).

## The warden's phases

`archive_warden` (behaviors/precursors.ron) reads `hull`, which counts the parts lost with sections shot off: the
heart holds about 60 % of the warden's hit points, the fore section 14 %, each wing 11 %, the bridges 1 % each. Whole
(and at 88 % or more) it keeps 95 m off (rule 4; 120 m while a ward is down, rule 3); below 88 % (the fore section
shot off, or both wings, or heavy damage) it circles at 35 m (rule 2); below 55 % it rams (rule 1). A section shot off
is a vessel of its own, whole by its own count, and starts over at range. The test
`the_warden_switches_its_tactics_as_its_sections_break_off` shoots the bridges through and checks each switch; the
`archive` determinism scenario shoots one bridge every 240 ticks in a radiating cluster and records the rules run.
