# The railgun and the salvager's checks, 20 Oct 2026

Measurements for [GDR-0129](../gdr/0129-blinded-cargo-leader-near-blink-button-and-the-railgun.md): the railgun's
numbers (a sidegrade beside the long-barrel cannon and the beam laser) and the `salvager` template with the new
behavior words. Load average 7–15 throughout (two other agents building).

## Method

- **Voyages:** the headless voyage runner (`cargo run --release -p sim --example voyage`), a mod pack (`--pack`) over
  the base content for each variant (a `parts/railgun.ron` with the variant's numbers, a `behaviors/escort.ron` with
  the salvager's rules), clear % = goal cleared and home.
- **Designs** (blueprints, not content): the rim skiff with its pulse lasers swapped (`cannon`: two
  reverse-engineered cannons; `long`: two long-barrel cannons over the gun and capacitor cells; `rail`: two railguns
  over the same cells, the hull one row longer so the 1×3 guns fit, their muzzles one cell ahead of the old nose) and
  the Halvex Runner with its two nose beams swapped (`long`: two long-barrel cannons in their cells; `rail`: two
  railguns, the nose one row longer).
- **Arena:** `arena fight` duels of the designs (200 seeds, 90 s) and `arena report` of the seven designs with six
  automatons and the sample mid ship (16 seeds in each starting position).

long-cannon Runner 1663, cannon skiff 1529, skiff 1411; the railgun is carried by 2 designs, picked by 29 % of the top
half (the long cannon 29 %, the beam laser 14 %), score 0.81 when used: no flag (DOMINANT needs a 50 % pick rate).

**Verdict: a sidegrade.** On the starter it trades the long cannon's steadiness for the hunter den (58 against 44 %)
and loses more elsewhere, worst at the Warden (13 against 36 %: a slow gun against a big hull); on the Runner it is
about level with the beams (better early, worse at the Warden) and far better than long cannons there. It loses the
duel to the long-cannon skiff and rates level with it.

## The salvager's checks

replaced by a pack (clear %, haul items median):

while enemies are near brings `yard_approach` back level with plain escorts; `halvex_drift` still costs 8 points.
The voyage runner's escorts keep `escort` (Niklas, 20 Oct 2026).
