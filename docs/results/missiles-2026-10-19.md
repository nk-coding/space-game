## Reading

- **Rockets are a sidegrade**: in the arena between the lasers and the cannons against gunners and lancers, best
  against the armoured hunter pack (a warhead hits several plates and what lies behind them); in the voyage runner
  above the lasers and below the cannons on Corran Reach. Head to head the rocket starter beats the laser starter.
- **Point defence counters missiles**: the laser starter against a lancer pack 4 → 47 of 60, a lone lancer 22 → 100 %;
  against gun fits nothing changes (the turrets take the nose slopes' place and shoot only small ships close in).
- **The lancer** is harder for the laser starter than a hunter (it lives in the hunting reaches, where the player
  should have cannons or point defence by then), easy for the cannon fit.

A trace of the first numbers (lasers against a lone lancer): the two ships flew side by side at 40 m; the laser
skiff's fixed guns bore 12 times in 28 s while the lancer's rockets, launched within half a radian and homing, struck
every salvo. The narrower launch cone and slower turn give misses; the light 0.4-damage rockets could not break the
hunters' plating (Corran Reach 0 % clear), so they became fewer and heavier.

## Follow-up: point defence's line of sight, flares, a lighter lancer (19 Oct 2026, later)

Same harness and seeds (`arena fight`, 60 seeds, 100 m, 120 s, one fit against a pack; the voyage runner on Corran
Reach, 120 seeds). A new fit, `../space-game-balance/fits/skiff_flares.ron`: the rim skiff with flare launchers for
its two nose slopes, flying the skirmisher with `when missiles_incoming >= 1 -> flare` on top.

- **Line of sight**: point defence shoots only what it sees (one ray cast back from the target to the muzzle; hulls,
  its own ship's included, and rocks block it). Lasers + PD against a lancer pack 47 → 46 of 60, the rest as before.
- **Lancer**: encounter first. The lancer pack without its raider (the old two-pod lancer alone) still left the
  laser starter 12 / 41 / 7, below a quarter, so the lancer itself carries one rocket pod now (an automaton plate
  block in the other's place) and the pack keeps its raider. The rocket pod is unchanged.
- **Flares**: 3 releases a fight, 6 s apart, of 3 decoys (14 m/s, 2.5 s, lure 12 m). They help less than point
  defence (limited charges, decoys only pull missiles near them) but take no power and no arc.
