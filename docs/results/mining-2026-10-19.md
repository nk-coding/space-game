# Mining balance, 19 Oct 2026 (GDR-0114)

What a mining run brings home against a fight-and-salvage run, with the voyage runner (`sim::voyage::runner`, which
mines since GDR-0114: a ship with a mining laser and hold room mines the nearest rock with ore within 300 m when
there is no fight or salvage; escorts with one fly `miner`) through the balance harness's voyage mode
(`../space-game-balance`, `--voyages … --fleets …`, two new columns: the mean length of the voyages that came
home and the materials *now* per minute of them). 120 seeds a row, 3 threads, load average about 1.7.

Fleets: `rim_skiff` (the starter: two pulse lasers, a scanner and one cargo bay, a hold of 4);
`skiff_miner` (the same with a mining laser for its right pulse laser and a second cargo bay for its scanner, a hold
of 8; `space-game-balance/mining/skiff_miner.ron`); and the skiff with the miner as its escort. The numbers are the
lead defaults of GDR-0114: cut 0.5 a second, iron rock 0.02 ore per m², ice 0.03, the smelter two ore for one.

On the quiet voyage the miner brings home 7.6 units a voyage (4.3 nickel-iron, 0.3 water ice) in 2.3 minutes against
the skiff's 4.6 in 1.9 (3.3 against 2.4 a minute). The smelter makes two ore into one steel or coolant, so counted at
what it gives the miner's haul is about 5.3 units, 2.3 a minute: what the fight-and-salvage run gives (2.4). Rare materials
(programmable matter, crystal, automaton plate) fall from 2.4 to 1.5 a voyage: the miner fights with one laser and
its hold fills with ore. The hold caps a run (8 units cut in 16 s); the ice fields lie farther than the scattered
iron, so the skiff fit brings home little ice. With the miner as an escort the fleet brings 11.3 units (6.1
nickel-iron), the skiff's whole fight-and-salvage haul and the ore on top.
