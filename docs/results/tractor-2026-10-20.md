# The tractor beam and the runner's tractor escorts, 20 Oct 2026

Measurements for [GDR-0131](../gdr/0131-the-tractor-beam.md). Load average 8–12 (two other agents building).

## Method

- The headless voyage runner (`cargo run --release -p sim --example voyage`), the rim skiff as flagship (closing
  pilot) with two escorts, seeds 1..80 and 1..320; clear = goal cleared and home; haul = items, median of the runs
  that came home.
- Escorts: `rim_skiff` (plain escorts, `escort`, as GDR-0123 and GDR-0129 measured); `sample_salvager` (the rim skiff
  with a tractor beam for its scanner and cargo bays for its rear armour, hold 12) flying `salvager`, the runner's new
  choice for escorts with a tractor; the same escorts flying `escort` (a pack overriding `salvager` with the escort's
  rules), to separate the beam from the behavior.

## Results

| escorts | `yard_approach` 80 | 320 | `halvex_drift` 80 | 320 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| rim skiffs, `escort` | 61, haul 5 | 232 (72 %), haul 5 | 45, haul 4 | 162 (51 %), haul 4 |
| sample salvagers, `salvager` (the runner now) | 53, haul 17 | 222 (69 %), haul 18 | 40, haul 19 | 152 (48 %), haul 17 |
| sample salvagers, `escort` | 56, haul 19 | 234 (73 %), haul 20 | 43, haul 17 | 150 (47 %), haul 16 |

- The beam is what brings the haul: escorts with one bring home three to four times the items (median 16–20 against
  4–5), most of it pulled in while they keep near the flagship, and programmable matter 2.2–3.8 a run against
  1.4–2.2.
- The `salvager` behavior adds little on top of the beam and costs a few goals on `yard_approach` (222 against 234 of
  320 with the same escorts flying `escort`; 10 fewer than plain rim skiffs); on `halvex_drift` it is level (152
  against 150). The tractor escorts trade a scanner and two armour blocks for the hold, so they lose a few more runs
  than plain rim skiffs (halvex 150–152 against 162).
- As decided, the runner's escorts with a tractor fly `salvager`. The numbers say `escort` would do as well; that is
  an open point for Niklas (GDR-0131).
