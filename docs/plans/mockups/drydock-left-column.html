Where armour goes on the left, and the new bottom bar

Every variant shares the same column: search, one line of category chips that scrolls sideways and jumps to its section, then every part in sections. The armour picker differs. Today there are 4 materials; the swatch rows also show 6 later ones (faded, made-up names) so you can judge how each picker holds about 10. The chips and swatches here are clickable.
